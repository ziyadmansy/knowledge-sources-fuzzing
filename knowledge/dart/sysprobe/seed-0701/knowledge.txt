9. Duplicate keys: last wins for all (probe 18 all accept with last "id":2).  
10. All implementations decode accepted documents identically (probes 3, 11, 14).  
11. Exception types differ: manual, json_serializable, and freezed mostly throw _TypeError or ArgumentError; built_value throws DeserializationError.
